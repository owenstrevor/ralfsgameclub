What we offer

Homework First

Daily academic support and supervision to help students stay on track and succeed.

Chess Academy

World-class chess instruction, led by experienced coaches and designed to help every student reach their full potential.

  • Professional instruction by experienced coaches
  • Personalized training by level
  • Tactics, strategy, and game analysis
  • Regular practice and mini tournaments
  • Preparation for competitions
  • Occasional visits from GM Luka Budislavejic

Gaming Lab

Organized gaming sessions, tournaments and team challenges in a safe and supervised space.

Board Games

Discover new games, practice strategy, teamwork and problem solving.

Literature Club

Each student will write a short story during the semester. At the end, we will publish a compilation of all the stories.

Visits from GM Luka Budislavejic

Students will have the opportunity to learn from and be inspired by Grandmaster Luka Budislavejic during his visits, simuls, lectures and special events.
