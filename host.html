Before you apply

What kinds of events are a good fit?

Welcoming community meetups, game nights, workshops, tournaments, school or nonprofit gatherings, and thoughtful local collaborations.

Is submitting a request a confirmed booking?

No. Your date is confirmed only after Ralf's reviews the request and replies with approval and final details.
