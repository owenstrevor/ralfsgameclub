Chess Program

Ralf's Chess Program brings world-class chess instruction to Santurce. Led by experienced coaches, it's designed to help every player reach their full potential — from first moves to tournament preparation. Everyone's welcome at the table.

What to expect

  • Professional instruction by experienced coaches
  • Personalized training by level
  • Tactics, strategy, and game analysis
  • Regular practice and mini tournaments
  • Preparation for competitions

Visits from GM Luka Budislavejic

Members will have the opportunity to learn from and be inspired by Grandmaster Luka Budislavejic during his visits, simuls, lectures and special events.

Chess for students

The Chess Academy is also at the heart of our After School Program for ages 8 – 16, with regular practice, personalized training and mini tournaments every week.
